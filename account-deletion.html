How to delete your WildHeavy account and what happens to your data

01 · How to Delete Your Account.

You can delete your WildHeavy account at any time from within the app. The process is immediate and irreversible.

Steps to delete from the app

  1. Open WildHeavy and tap your avatar in the top right
  2. Tap Account to open Settings
  3. Find the ACCOUNT group and tap Delete on the Delete Account row
  4. Choose what happens to your posts: Leave them up, anonymously or Remove my posts
  5. Tap Delete My Account
  6. Tick the confirmation box, then tap Continue
  7. Type DELETE and tap "Delete My Account"
  8. Your account is deleted and you are signed out immediately
Note: If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing support@wildheavy.com from the email address associated with your account. Tell us which option you want for your posts. We will process your request within 48 hours.

02 · What Happens to Your Posts.

When you delete your account, you choose what happens to your posts (your rankings, Menu Cards, Routes and the photos in them):

Leave them up, anonymously.

Your posts stay with no name on them. They count toward Top Rank at one tenth of a normal vote, and toward popular dishes, dish scores and restaurant briefings like any other post. They no longer appear in the Feed, in Explore or in restaurant page lists, and photos show no credit. Anyone who saved one, reposted it or holds a share link can still open it, where it appears as "Deleted User".

Remove my posts.

Your rankings, Menu Cards, Routes and their photos are deleted, along with notes other people left on them. Anyone who saved them loses them.

03 · What Happens to Everything Else.

Either way, we delete:

  • Your profile (name, username, bio, photo, location, dietary restrictions) and any badges
  • Your email and password. Your email is replaced with a placeholder that cannot be reversed, and the account can never be signed into again. You are signed out right away, and any other device you were signed in on within an hour
  • Your followers and the people you follow, and your likes, saves and reposts
  • Notes and replies you left
  • Your notifications, notification settings and push notification tokens
  • Your taste profile, visit history, stamps and other account settings
  • Crews you created, and your membership in other Crews
  • Unpublished drafts

Either way, we keep these records with no name attached:

  • Reports, moderation actions and abuse-prevention records (see Section 5.3)
  • Item names and prices read from receipts you scanned
  • App usage statistics, such as which features are used and where sign-up steps are skipped. Records of failed posting attempts are deleted after 90 days
  • Corrections you suggested to restaurant or dish information
After deletion, your username and email can be used to create a new account, which starts empty. Deleted content cannot be restored or reconnected to you.

04 · Questions?

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at support@wildheavy.com.

For full details on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
